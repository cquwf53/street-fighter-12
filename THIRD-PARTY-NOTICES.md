Street Fighter II, its characters, names, original artwork and visual designs are associated with Capcom and their respective rights holders. This independent HTML tribute is not an official Capcom release or a ROM emulator. Attribution does not imply ownership or relicensing of the original artwork.

## Selection portraits and map
Source: https://github.com/trevzix/street-fighter-2-selection

## Ryu / Ken animation sheets and Ken stage
Source: https://github.com/g3th3x/Street-fighter-2-Clone
Included: images/ryu.png, images/ken.png, images/ken-stage.png, images/misc.png, images/decals.png, with frame coordinates adapted to this project's existing renderer.

Title treatment, custom bitmap type, interface assembly, remaining procedural fighters / airbase, effects, and synthesized audio are implemented in this project. Original resource files and intermediate build scripts have been retained locally.
